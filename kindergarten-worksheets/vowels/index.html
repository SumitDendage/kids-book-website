Vowels Worksheets for Kindergarten

Every “why” deserves a “wow”.

Children learn the five vowels a, e, i, o and u and their short sounds. Here are all 10 sheets in this topic, each with a preview of the page. The ones marked Free open without paying.

All Vowels sheets

  • Vowels 1 — find and colour the vowels

    Find the vowels a, e, i, o and u among the letters and colour them red.

    Free · 2 pages

  • Vowels 2 — match the beginning vowel

    Match each picture to the vowel its name begins with: owl, apple, igloo, uncle and egg.

    Free · 2 pages

  • Vowels 3 — match the beginning vowel: more pictures

    Match each picture to the vowel its name begins with: unicorn, insect, olive, astronaut and eye.

    Free · 2 pages

  • Vowels 4 — count the vowels

    Read each word (avocado, apple, igloo, orange, umbrella), find its vowels and count them.

    ₹19 · 2 pages

  • Vowels 5 — count the vowels: more words

    Read each word (elephant, sun, alligator, envelope, ice cream), find its vowels and count them.

    ₹19 · 2 pages

  • Vowels 6 — fill in the missing vowel

    Look at each picture and write the missing vowel (a, e, i, o or u) to finish the word.

    ₹19 · 2 pages

  • Vowels 7 — fill in the missing vowel: more words

    Look at five more pictures and write the missing vowel to finish each word.

    ₹19 · 2 pages

  • Vowels 8 — short a words

    Write each short a word (map, tap, can, van, fan and more) under the correct picture.

    ₹19 · 2 pages
